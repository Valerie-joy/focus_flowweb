1Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how FocusFlow (“FocusFlow”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use the FocusFlow mobile application (the “App”) and this website (the “Website”).

FocusFlow is an attention assessment tool. It helps you understand your attention patterns through interactive assessments, camera-based attention tracking, and personalized insights. It is not a substitute for professional medical diagnosis, and its results are not a diagnosis of ADHD or any other condition.

Because the App can involve your device camera and, if you choose to provide it, information about an ADHD diagnosis, this policy explains those areas in particular detail.

To confirm: the full legal name and address of the person or organization responsible for your information (the data controller or personal information controller), and the laws this policy is written under (for example, the Philippines' Data Privacy Act of 2012, the EU/UK GDPR, or US state privacy laws, depending on where the App is offered).

2Information we collect

Based on the App's current design, FocusFlow may collect the following categories of information. Each is described in more detail in the sections below.

  • Profile information you enter during registration, such as your name, age, and email address.
  • ADHD diagnosis status, and an optional document you choose to upload.
  • Content preferences, such as the genres you select (for example Drama, Music, Gaming, Animation, Finance, Sci-Fi, or Education).
  • Camera and attention-tracking data generated during calibration and the assessment.
  • Assessment results, including attention metrics, your attention archetype, confidence levels, recommendations, and reports.
  • Communications, such as the report sent to your email address and any messages you send us.

To confirm: whether the App also collects device or technical information (such as device model, operating system, app version, IP address, crash logs, or usage events), and if so, what is collected and why.

3Personal information

During registration, the App asks for:

  • Your name, so the App can address you and personalize your experience.
  • Your age, which the App uses to tailor the assessment to your age group.
  • Your email address, which is used to send your assessment report.

You can choose not to provide this information, but some features, such as receiving your report by email, may not work without it.

To confirm: whether accounts use a password or a sign-in provider (and which one), whether the email address is verified, and whether profile information is stored only on the device or also on a server.

4ADHD-related information and uploaded documents

The App asks whether you have an official ADHD diagnosis. If you answer yes, you can choose to upload a supporting document (the App accepts PDF, JPG, or PNG files). Uploading a document is optional, and you can continue the assessment without one.

Information about a diagnosis, and any medical document you upload, is health information. Under many privacy laws it is treated as sensitive personal information and may only be processed with your explicit consent or another specific legal basis.

5Camera permissions and attention tracking data

The App asks for permission to use your device's front camera. The camera is used to calibrate the App to your eyes, device, and position, and then to estimate where you are looking during the assessment.

From this, the App derives attention signals such as fixation time, a focus percentage, and how often your gaze moves away from the content.

Camera access is controlled by your device's permission system. You can decline, or later turn camera access off in your device settings. Without camera access, camera-based calibration and attention tracking will not be available.

To confirm: (1) whether any video, images, or frames from the camera are recorded, saved, or uploaded; (2) whether processing happens only on your device, on a server, or through a third-party service; (3) exactly which derived data (for example gaze coordinates or attention metrics) are stored, and where; (4) whether facial images or derived data could count as biometric information under applicable law; (5) whether any camera-derived data is used to train or improve AI models; and (6) whether any part of the assessment works without the camera.

Check the App's wording: the camera permission screen in the current design says “We do NOT record video”, “Only gaze data is processed”, and “Your data is private & secure”. FocusFlow uses the information described above to:

  • create and manage your profile;
  • calibrate the camera and run the attention assessment;
  • select assessment content based on the genres you choose;
  • analyze attention signals and generate your attention metrics, archetype, and recommendations;
  • send your assessment report to your email address;
  • review an uploaded document, if you provide one, for the purpose described in section 4;
  • respond to your questions and requests; and
  • meet legal obligations.

To confirm: whether information is also used to maintain and improve the App, to train or evaluate AI models, for research, or to send product updates or marketing emails, and the legal basis or consent relied on for each use.

7AI processing and assessment results

FocusFlow uses AI-based analysis to turn attention signals from your assessment into results, including attention metrics, an attention archetype (for example “Toy Tornado”), a confidence level, and personalized recommendations.

These results are estimates. They can be affected by things like lighting, camera quality, your position, the content shown, and how you felt during the session. They describe patterns observed in one assessment and are not a medical diagnosis. Talk to a qualified healthcare professional about any concerns regarding attention or ADHD.

FocusFlow results are not intended to be used to make decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.
