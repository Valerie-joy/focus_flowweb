What is FocusFlow?

FocusFlow is a mobile app that helps you see how your attention actually behaves, not just how it feels.

Through a short, camera-guided assessment built around content you enjoy, FocusFlow looks at signals such as how long your gaze holds, when it drifts, and how engagement changes between formats. It turns those signals into an attention profile you can understand and act on.

Knowing your patterns can help you pick formats that keep you engaged, plan work in sizes that suit you, and have better-informed conversations, including with a healthcare professional if you decide to seek one.

FocusFlow helps you

  • Notice when and how your attention shifts
  • Compare your engagement across types of content
  • Get practical suggestions matched to your pattern
  • Bring a clearer picture to a conversation with a professional

FocusFlow does not

  • Diagnose ADHD or any other condition
  • Replace evaluation by a qualified clinician
  • Recommend, start, or change any medication
  • Treat one session as a definitive measure of you

How FocusFlow works

Six steps take you from a new profile to a personalized picture of your attention. Everything happens in the FocusFlow mobile app.

  1. Create your profile

    Tell FocusFlow your name, age, and email. You'll also be asked whether you have an official ADHD diagnosis; uploading a supporting document is optional.

    • Name
    • Age
    • Email
    • Diagnosis status
    • Optional document

  2. Select your interests

    Pick the content genres you enjoy so the assessment uses material you'd actually choose to watch.

    • Drama
    • Music
    • Gaming
    • Animation
    • Finance
    • Sci-Fi
    • Education

  3. Enable and calibrate your camera

    Allow camera access, then follow a moving dot with your eyes. Calibration helps the app read your gaze for your device and seating position.

  4. Complete the attention assessment

    Watch a series of content segments while the app tracks attention signals such as fixation time, focus, and moments of looking away.

  5. Analyze attention patterns

    The app processes your session, comparing engagement across genres and detecting patterns over time.

  6. Discover your results and recommendations

    See your attention archetype and personalized recommendations, then receive a detailed report by email.

Designed around consent

FocusFlow involves sensitive things: your camera and, if you choose to share it, information about an ADHD diagnosis. The app is designed to ask clearly, explain why, and keep you informed about what happens with your information.

Read the privacy policy

  • Permission first

    Camera access is requested with an explanation of why it's needed before the assessment begins.

  • Optional documents

    Uploading a diagnosis document is optional. You can continue the assessment without one.

  • Clear about limits

    Results describe attention patterns and are presented as insights, not as a medical diagnosis.

  • A published policy

    The privacy policy sets out what information is collected and how it's used, so you can decide with the full picture.
